# Problem

Everything here is a hypothesis, thresholds included. We have not talked to customers yet.

## Problem statement

In webinar Q&A, the speaker answers the first or loudest questions, not the ones most of the audience cares about. Participants have no quick, anonymous way to show which question matters, so Q&A time is spent badly.

## Hypotheses for version one

1. At least 30% of attendees ask or vote. Kill: under 15%.
2. At least 2 of the top 5 voted questions were not among the first 5 asked. Kill: ranking equals arrival order.
3. The host answers at least 3 of the top 5 live. Kill: the host returns to the chat.

## Left out of version one

- Image paste or upload, the room's top wish. We only count questions mentioning a slide or picture.
- Logins
- Threaded replies
- Zoom and Teams integration
- Exports
