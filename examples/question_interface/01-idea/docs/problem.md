# PitchPulse: problem and hypotheses

All of this is hypothesis; no customer interviews yet.

## Problem (hypothesis)

A solo trainer or consultant runs a live webinar for 30 to 200 people on a tool without good Q&A. In the Q&A block, questions scroll past in the chat, so the speaker answers the first or loudest ones, not the ones most attendees want answered.

## Hypotheses

1. **Joining is easy.** Wrong if under 30% of attendees ask or upvote.
2. **Votes show a clear priority.** Wrong if the top three questions get under half of all votes.
3. **The speaker follows the ranking.** Wrong if they answer fewer than three of the top five.

## Left out of version 1

- Login, accounts and names: a voter is a random cookie.
- Co-hosts and roles: one host moderates with a PIN.
- Replies, comments, polls, downvotes, duplicate merging.
- Analytics, exports, Zoom or Teams integration.
